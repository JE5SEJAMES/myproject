About Me

Welcome! I am a veteran technology professional with 17 years of experience at my company, currently serving as the Software/Applications Development Manager for the past 4 years. Prior to this role, I spent 10 years as a Systems Support Specialist troubleshooting complex software used to operate difficult manufacturing equipment. That decade on the front lines gave me a deep, practical understanding of real-world user experience and software compatibility. Outside of managing my 50+ hour workweeks and family life, I am also a part-time student pursuing my Bachelor of Applied Science in Information Technology online.

This website is a project of the IT3203 course, designed as a clean, straightforward resource detailing the history of the web browser, the importance of open W3C standards, and the core request-response architecture that makes cross-browser development possible.

This is a class project. The class website is: https://ksuweb.github.io/IT3203/
